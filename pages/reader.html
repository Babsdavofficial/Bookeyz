CHAPTER 1

The Festival Before the Storm

Beast World: The Fire Dragon Quest

Chapter Artwork

The Ember Kingdom had never looked more alive.

Flags stretched across the streets, merchants filled the markets, and people gathered from every corner of the kingdom for the annual festival.

Music echoed through the city while soldiers stood at the gates, watching the crowds.

At the heart of the kingdom stood the royal palace, where King Valoron watched the celebration from the great balcony.

Beside him stood Queen Elira and Princess Sofia.

Everything appeared peaceful.

But far beyond the kingdom's walls, something ancient had begun to stir.

The festival continued.

The people laughed.

And nobody knew that the first sign of the coming storm was already approaching.
